Intro

👋 Hey! I'm Yevhen

I'm an infrastructure and performance testing leader based in Lviv, Ukraine, with 25+ years of experience across IT operations, infrastructure architecture, and the processes that keep software organisations running.

Today I lead the Performance Testing Service at SoftServe, which I built from the ground up: the service offering, the development roadmap, the training program and the initial team — and 80+ delivered performance testing projects since.

Before that I spent over a decade running IT infrastructure and operations for software and e-commerce companies, leading teams of up to 32 people across networking, virtualization, storage, monitoring and information security.

I care about efficient, well-architected systems and clear processes. I've introduced ITIL-based IT processes, built monitoring covering thousands of services and hundreds of hosts, and led large infrastructure projects — from server room and datacenter builds to full network migrations and VoIP rollouts that cut telephony costs dramatically.

Budgeting matters to me as much as architecture: I've owned CAPEX/OPEX planning and driven efficiency through consolidation, while keeping services reliable for the business.

I also enjoy developing the people around me. I've built training programs from scratch, grown teams from their first hires into established departments, and lectured at Lviv Polytechnic National University.

I hold an MSc in Applied Mathematics and Informatics from Ivan Franko National University of Lviv, along with certifications from Cisco (CCNA, CCNP), VMware (VCP) and Microsoft (MCP).

In my spare time I'm usually in my homelab — a three-node Proxmox cluster running Home Assistant and TrueNAS, on a MikroTik network I've segmented into VLANs, with the occasional midnight outage I caused myself just to understand something better. Twenty-five years in, I still like keeping my own hands on the infrastructure. And away from the keyboard, I travel as much as I can, ideally toward water: yachting is my favourite way to be completely unreachable for a few days.
